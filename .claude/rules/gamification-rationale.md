Seven mechanics, each grounded in learning theory (Paper 3, Section 4):

| Mechanic | Theory basis | Key constraint |
|----------|-------------|---------------|
| XP + level progression | SDT competence, Flow goal clarity | All computed locally; no server needed |
| Mastery badges | Abramovich et al. 2013 — badges must reflect substantive competency | Conditions tied to real learning milestones, not participation |
| Streak + freeze tokens | SDT autonomy — connectivity failure is environmental, not personal (PR9) | ≥1 token/7 days to match documented outage frequency in conflict zones |
| Progress bars + dashboards | SDT competence, transparent goal tracking | Visible on home and progress screens |
| Module unlocking | Autonomy-directed exploration + scaffolded progression | Sequential unlock; diagnostic can bypass intro modules (PR12) |
| Conflict-handling prompts | Productive struggle / desirable difficulties (Sinha & Kapur, 2021) | Triggered on repeated errors; offer hints and alternative explanations |
| Opt-in cohort leaderboard | SDT relatedness without competitive pressure | Opt-in only; suppressed <10 members; display names anonymised (First L.) |

**Critical:** No mechanic should ever reduce a learner's standing due to connectivity or power outage (PR9). Validate this before changing any streak, XP, or badge logic.
